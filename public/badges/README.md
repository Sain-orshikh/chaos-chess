# Badge artwork

Fourteen self-contained SVG assets, composed from the existing Chaos Chess pieces and brand marks. The shield, clipped corners, charcoal surfaces, ivory pieces, and red accents follow the approved badge design.

- Canvas: `126 × 144` viewBox; default display dimensions `168 × 192`.
- Profile and match display: `34 × 39` preserves the aspect ratio.
- Transparent outside the shield. No scripts, external images, or font dependencies; rank and milestone marks are vector paths.
- `manifest.json` contains stable IDs, display names, public descriptions, categories, and asset URLs. It describes artwork only; it does not implement award or leaderboard logic.
- Three Musketiers has a visible name and a null description. Display “Description hidden” for its description; do not expose its challenge through tooltips or accessible labels.
- On the Rise uses the requested 600 Elo milestone.

Leaderboard artwork progresses from The Hundred (Top 100), to Podium (Top 3), Heir Apparent (Top 2), and Sovereign (#1). Rank marks and increasing trim distinguish tiers without relying on color alone.

The original piece art remains unchanged. The milestone knight is centered within the shield; its upward chevron sits symmetrically behind it.
